I am Hanfeng Wu(吴瀚枫), currently a joint PhD student in BMW Group and Karlsruhe Institute of Technology(KIT) supervised by [Prof. Christoph Stiller](https://www.mrt.kit.edu/mitarbeiter_stiller.php) I obtained my Master's and Bachelor's degree in computer science at TU Munich. I conducted my Master's thesis at ETH Zürich supervised by [Prof. Konrad Schindler](https://prs.igp.ethz.ch/group/people/person-detail.schindler.html) and [Prof. Stefan Leutenegger](https://srl.cit.tum.de/members/leuteneg) from TU Munich. Before that, I was a Research Intern at Huawei Munich Research Center and a Software Engineer Intern at Apple Munich.

My research interests lie in End-to-End Autonomous Driving and computer vision particularly in 3D scene understanding and neural rendering.
